Meet your teacher

Hi, I'm Del.

I teach a range of asana practices — from the steady discipline of Ashtanga to the deep stillness of Yin, dynamic Vinyasa, and strength and mobility work built to carry off the mat and into daily life.

Every class is beginner-friendly without being basic — clear cues, real modifications, and a pace that respects your breath over your ego.

What we practice

Six ways to move this week

Each class stands on its own — drop into whichever style fits how your body feels today, live on Zoom or on your own time via YouTube.

Vinyasa

Breath-linked movement that builds heat and flow. A dynamic sequence that still leaves room to modify.

Beginner friendly

Strength

Bodyweight and asana-based strength work, building stability and power from the ground up.

All levels

Mobility

Joint-focused movement to build range and ease, so strength and flexibility work together.

All levels

Ashtanga

A traditional, structured sequence practiced with consistent order — steady, disciplined, and grounding.

Some experience helpful

Yin

Long-held, passive postures that target connective tissue and invite deep stillness.

All levels

Pose Workshops

A deep dive into one posture or peak pose at a time — alignment, drills, and how to build toward it.
